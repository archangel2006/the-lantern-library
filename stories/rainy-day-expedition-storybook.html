Nell and the Great Indoor Expedition

A rainy-day adventure

Scroll to turn the page.

It had rained for three days. Nell had read all her books, built all her blocks, and counted every button in the button jar, twice.

“There is nothing to do,” she told the window. The window, rather rudely, kept on raining.

Then Nell had an idea, which is the best thing to have on a rainy day. “We’re not stuck indoors,” she said. “We’re on an expedition.”

She drew a map, put a colander on her head, and found her yellow raincoat, just in case.

The first challenge was the Carpet River, which was wide and blue and full of crocodiles. (One crocodile, made of felt.)

Nell hopped across on the cushions, one, two, three, four, and only the last one wobbled.

Next came Mount Sofa, the tallest mountain in the house. It took three sighs and a lot of puffing, but at the top, Nell planted her flag, which was a wooden spoon.

From up there, the whole living room looked small.

The map said the treasure was in the Cave of Doom, which is the space under the dining table, with a blanket for a roof.

From inside the dark came a low, rumbling sound. And then two yellow eyes opened.

“It’s a tiger,” whispered Nell. The tiger stepped out into the light and said, “Mrrow?”

It was Mochi, the cat, who had been guarding the cave for an hour, and who was only a little bit of a tiger.

Together they crept inside. And there, at the very back, was the treasure: a tin of ginger biscuits.

Nell had hidden it there herself, last Tuesday, and completely forgotten. Which, if you think about it, is the very best kind of treasure.

By the time the rain stopped, there was a rainbow over the garden and two very tired explorers on the sofa, sharing the last biscuit.

“Best expedition ever,” said Nell. Mochi agreed, in tiger.
