Gus Has the Hiccups

A very loud story from Crumb Hollow

Scroll to turn the page.

In the village of Crumb Hollow, everything smelled of bread, and everyone was very busy. Busiest of all was Marigold, the baker’s daughter, who got up before the roosters.

One Tuesday, a dragon landed in the village square, very softly, because he was very polite. His name was Gus.

“Sorry to bother you,” he said. “I seem to have the hiccups.” Then came a small hic! and a small fire jumped out of his mouth and set the mayor’s hat alight.

Everyone tried to help. The mayor said, “Hold your breath!” So Gus held it until he turned the colour of a plum and fell over.

The blacksmith said, “Drink water upside down!” So Gus drank the whole village pond. He did not stop hiccupping, but the fish were very surprised.

The baker said, “A good scare will do it!” So everyone hid behind the barrels, and when Gus came round the corner, they jumped out and shouted, “BOO!”

Gus, who was a dragon, thought this was adorable. He went hic! with delight, and all the barrels turned to toast.

Only Marigold, with flour on her nose, stood quietly watching. And she noticed something no one else had.

Every time Gus hiccupped, the flame was exactly the right heat for baking. Not too hot. Not too cool. Just right for bread.

“Excuse me, Gus,” she said. “Could you hiccup a little to the left?”

Hic! Three trays of loaves came out golden, crackly and perfect. It was the best bread in the history of Crumb Hollow, and it had taken less than a minute.

By Friday, Crumb Hollow had so much bread that the villagers held a party for it. Gus wore a tall white hat that Marigold had sewn from a tablecloth.

Nobody was sure when the hiccups would stop, and nobody asked.

Every morning at five, a loud hic! rolls over the hill, and that is how Crumb Hollow knows breakfast is ready.

Gus says he will miss the hiccups when they finally go. Marigold says not to worry, because so far, they haven’t.
